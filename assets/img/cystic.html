Acne Type: Cystic Acne

Based on your answers to our acne quiz, you most likely have cystic acne.

Don't be scared!
You might ask why it happens.

Cystic acne happens when there is deep inflammation inside your oil glands and hair follicles. Oil, dead skin cells, and bacteria get trapped, causing large, painful bumps under the skin.

Lifestyle Tips

  • Try to regularly use sunscreen.
  • DON'T pick or squeeze!
  • Change pillows and sheets regularly.
