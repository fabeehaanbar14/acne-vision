Acne Type: Hormonal Acne

Based on your answers to our acne quiz, you most likely have hormonal acne. Hormonal acne is triggered by fluctuations in hormones—especially androgens. It commonly appears along the jawline, chin, and neck, and can also show up on the back.

Hormonal acne often flares during stress or hormonal shifts—don’t worry, you’re not alone!

Lifestyle Tips

  • Include healthy fats in your diet
  • Find ways to reduce stress—your skin will thank you
